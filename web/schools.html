Myth · Conjurers

Make imagination your strategy.

Wizard101’s Myth school description frames Myth around imagination and the power to bring ideas to life. It describes Conjurers calling legendary creatures by their True Names and building a toolkit around minions.

A second path to consider

Shadow · Shadow magic

Take a calculated risk.

Shadow transformations trade a tactical drawback for a specialized advantage. Shadow Shrike leans into offense, Shadow Sentinel into protection, and Shadow Seraph into healing; check their current in-game effects before building around one.

Explore Shadow spells
01

Choose a form for the fight

Use an offensive, defensive, or healing form to support the plan your team needs—not just because a Shadow pip is available.

02

Plan around the drawback

Shadow forms can carry backlash or other trade-offs. Time the transformation for a turn when you can make its benefit count.

03

Keep a conventional option

Shadow pips and spell availability are not guaranteed. Keep dependable school spells in your deck so your strategy still works without a form.

Turn the school identity into a deck plan

A Conjurer’s toolkit

01

Make room for minions

The official description highlights four minion types with different functions. Keep your deck flexible enough to support the summon that fits the fight.

02

Support your summon

Myth’s described toolkit includes spells that buff, heal, and protect minions. Think about support cards alongside direct attacks when planning a setup.

03

Pack an answer to defenses

The school description also calls out Myth’s ability to remove an opponent’s defenses. Consider keeping a utility option ready for shield-heavy encounters.
